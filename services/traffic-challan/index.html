Traffic Challan Check Kya Hai?

E-Challan portal se aap apne vehicle ya driving license par pending traffic challan check kar sakte hain aur online pay kar sakte hain. Speed camera aur traffic police dono ke challans yahan dikhte hain.

Speed cameras aur traffic police ke challans ab online check hote hain. Samay par pay karein otherwise court notice aa sakta hai.

Iske Fayde

  • Ghar baithe challan check kar sakte hain
  • Online pay karke court se bach sakte hain
  • Vehicle number se sabhi challans dekhein
  • Payment ki receipt instant milti hai

Kaise Karein – Step by Step

  1. 1E-Challan portal par jaayein
  2. 2Vehicle number ya DL number daalein
  3. 3Pending challans ki list dikhegi
  4. 4Pay karein online
  5. 5Receipt download karein

Zaruri Tips

  • Vehicle registration number sahi dalein
  • Pending challans time par pay karein
  • Challan pay karne ke baad receipt save karein

Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Challan pay nahi kiya to kya hoga?

Court notice aa sakta hai, vehicle impound ho sakta hai aur DL suspend ho sakta hai.

Purana challan ka kaise pata lagayein?

Vehicle number se E-Challan portal par search karein – sabhi pending challans dikh jaayenge.

Hum Aapki Madad Kar Sakte Hain

Agar aapko Traffic Challan Check me koi problem aa rahi hai ya aap khud se nahi kar pa rahe, to Nain CSC Center (Nain Photostate Danoda), Jind par aayein. Hamare trained operators aapka poora kaam karwa denge - form filling se lekar final submission tak.
