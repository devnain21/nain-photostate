Digital Seva (CSC) Portal Kya Hai?

Digital Seva portal Common Service Centers (CSC) ka official platform hai jahan se 300+ digital services provide ki jati hain. PAN card, Aadhaar services, banking, insurance, electricity bill payment aur bahut kuch yahan se hota hai.

CSC centers sarkar ka digital extension hain jo gramin areas tak services pahunchate hain. Banking, insurance, education, aur healthcare sab CSC se accessible hai.

Iske Fayde

  • 300+ digital services ek window par
  • Rural areas me bhi government services available
  • Trained operators se madad milti hai
  • Banking aur insurance services bhi

Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawal

CSC center par kya kya hota hai?

PAN card, Aadhaar update, bank account, insurance, bill payment, government forms aur bahut kuch CSC center par hota hai.

CSC services ki fees kitni hoti hai?

Har service ki alag fees hai. Kuch free hain aur kuch me nominal charges lagte hain.

Hum Aapki Madad Kar Sakte Hain

Agar aapko Digital Seva (CSC) Portal me koi problem aa rahi hai ya aap khud se nahi kar pa rahe, to Nain CSC Center (Nain Photostate Danoda), Jind par aayein. Hamare trained operators aapka poora kaam karwa denge - form filling se lekar final submission tak.
