Farmer ID (Kisan Registry) Kya Hai?

Haryana Kisan Registry me registration karna sabhi farmers ke liye zaruri hai. Farmer ID se aap PM Kisan, fasal bima, mandi payment aur sabhi kisan yojanaon ka labh le sakte hain. Nain CSC Center se registration hota hai.

Haryana Farmer Registry me registered kisan ko ek unique ID milti hai jo sabhi agriculture schemes ke liye single identity ka kaam karti hai.

Iske Fayde

  • Ek ID se sabhi kisan yojanaon ka labh
  • Subsidy seedha bank account me aati hai
  • Fasal bima aur mandi registration easy
  • Digital identity se paperwork kam

Kaise Karein – Step by Step

  1. 1Kisan Registry portal par jaayein
  2. 2Aadhaar number se registration karein
  3. 3Zameen ki details bharein
  4. 4Bank account link karein
  5. 5Farmer ID generate hogi

Zaruri Tips

  • Jamabandi me zameen kisan ke naam honi chahiye
  • Aadhaar aur bank account pehle se link karein
  • Meri Fasal Mera Byora par bhi registration karein

Aksar Puchhe Jaane Wale Sawal

Farmer ID kisliye zaruri hai?

Bina Farmer ID ke koi bhi kisan yojana ka labh nahi mil sakta – PM Kisan, fasal bima, mandi payment sab ke liye zaruri hai.

Farmer ID me zameen ka record kaise add karein?

Jamabandi nakal ki details bharne se zameen ka record automatically link ho jaata hai.

Hum Aapki Madad Kar Sakte Hain

Agar aapko Farmer ID (Kisan Registry) me koi problem aa rahi hai ya aap khud se nahi kar pa rahe, to Nain CSC Center (Nain Photostate Danoda), Jind par aayein. Hamare trained operators aapka poora kaam karwa denge - form filling se lekar final submission tak.
